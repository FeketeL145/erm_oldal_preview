Formula Student

Formula Student is the most established educational motorsport competition in the world. University students design, build, test, and race a formula-style prototype vehicle while pitching it to an imaginary market on an international stage.

A Formula Student event spans four rigorous days. The first two days focus entirely on technical inspection and strict safety compliance (scrutineering). Passing these tests qualifies the car to compete in the dynamic track events over the following two days. The challenges are divided into static and dynamic disciplines.

Static events test engineering design rationale, cost analysis, and commercial viability. Students present their Engineering Design defence, Business Plan Presentation, and a comprehensive Cost and Manufacturing report to judges from leading automotive and motorsport companies. These static disciplines account for 40% of the total score.

Dynamic events push our car to its physical limits: Acceleration (75m sprint), Skidpad (figure-8 lateral grip test), Autocross (single-lap sprint against the clock), and the crown jewel of the competition: the 22 km Endurance & Efficiency race, testing vehicle reliability, thermal management, and energy consumption under maximum racing strain.

Our members acquire practical engineering expertise in EV systems, aerodynamics, vehicle dynamics, carbon composites, and telemetry. We cultivate authentic teamwork and transfer academic theory directly into real-world racing engineering, building high-value pathways between ambitious students and leading tier-1 industry partners.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about joining the team, time commitment, and opportunities.

There is no strict minimum time requirement to join the team. However, experience shows that 3-4 hours per week during the onboarding and training period is essential to acquire the necessary skills. As your responsibilities grow, your time commitment will naturally increase. Most engineering and business tasks can also be completed remotely after classes.

An average university course lasts 7 semesters. You can only compete in Formula Student while holding active student status. Since you are learning during this time, we do not expect prior industry experience—only enthusiasm, dedication, and openness to learn. Joining early gives you the best chance to master key skills and take on leadership roles.

In short, being in the team actually helps your studies! Senior members actively mentor younger students with coursework, exams, and projects. You can complete your mandatory university internships with us and write your BSc/MSc thesis or TDK scientific research on your actual race car engineering projects.

Just like a professional motorsport team, we rely heavily on our business department. Managing sponsor acquisitions, financial budgets, logistics, PR, and social media is vital to our success. In addition, business students prepare and deliver the official Business Plan Presentation at Formula Student competitions. Technical knowledge is not required.

Formula Student trains engineers who know how to apply modern theory to actual hardware. From battery pack thermal modelling and custom PCBs to composite prepreg manufacturing and telemetry analysis, you gain competitive industry skills that are virtually impossible to acquire in a traditional classroom setting.

Yes! Through our internal scholarship program, active members receive financial grants proportional to their contribution and diligence each semester. Furthermore, research papers and TDK work created within the team qualify for major university and national scholarships (Universitas Miskolcensis, ÚNKP, National Higher Education Scholarship).

Any student with active university enrollment status. Students from all faculties—Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, IT, Economics, Materials Science, and Law—are warmly welcome!

We hold recruitment rounds each semester announced on our website and social channels. You can apply online via our form, followed by an informal in-person interview. If you missed the official recruitment window, feel free to contact us directly at any time.

Responsibilities vary depending on your position and availability. The higher your role in the technical or management hierarchy, the more people count on you. During your first year, the emphasis is almost entirely on learning, with experienced mentors guiding your progress every step of the way.

We do not recruit dedicated drivers who only wish to sit in the cockpit. However, we regularly organize internal test sessions and "fun-drive" track days where everyone who contributed to building the car gets the chance to test it behind the wheel.
